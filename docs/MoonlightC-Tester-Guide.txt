MOONLIGHTC TESTER GUIDE
About 20 minutes. If something breaks, send a screenshot and one line on what you clicked.

BEFORE YOU START: GET A FREE AI KEY (2 min)
1. Go to https://aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with Google.
2. Click "Create API key" and copy it. It's free, no card needed.

1. WEBSITE (2 min)
1. Open https://moonlightc.vercel.app and scroll to the bottom.
2. Click every page in the top menu. Note anything broken, slow or ugly.

2. WEB APP (5 min)
1. Click "Open web app".
2. Click the key icon ("Models and keys"), pick Gemini, paste your key, click "Check key and connect".
3. Ask it something. Then switch to a different model mid-chat and ask a follow-up.
4. Refresh the page. Is your chat still there?

3. DESKTOP APP (5 min)
1. On the home page click "Download for Windows" (or Mac).
2. Install it.
   Windows: if it says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   Mac: if it's blocked, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".
3. Open it, connect your key, and send a chat.

4. CODE (3 min)
1. In the app, open "Code".
2. Type: "Make a tip calculator." Does it build something you can use?

5. CHROME EXTENSION (3 min)
1. On the website's Download page, click "Download the extension" and unzip it.
2. In Chrome go to chrome://extensions, turn on "Developer mode" (top right),
   click "Load unpacked", and pick the unzipped folder.
3. Open any website, click the MoonlightC icon, choose "Share this tab".
4. In MoonlightC ask: "What's on the tab I shared?"

WHAT TO SEND BACK
- Your device (Windows, Mac, phone) and browser.
- Anything that broke, with a screenshot.
- The one thing that felt most confusing.
